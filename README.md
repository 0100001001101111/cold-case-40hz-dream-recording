# COLD CASE EEG-03 — A steady 40-hertz hum in one public dream recording that nobody can source

Family: consciousness-physiology  |  Disposition: **POSTABLE_AS_COLD_CASE**  |  claim_authority=NONE
Members: dream-zhang-40hz-source-v1, dream-zhang-40hz-component-map-v1, dream-zhang-hf-contamination-v1, dream-zhang-hf-decomposition-v1, dream-zhang-hf-time-frequency-v1, dream-40hz-source-isolated-v1, dream-40hz-labeled-artifact-v1

## The case
One subject-night in the public DREAM sleep-EEG database carries a persistent, regionally structured 40-hertz component with an exceptional spectral share. Forty hertz is the classic gamma-band frequency people link to conscious awareness - so what IS this: brain, muscle, electrode, or gear?

## How it died
Seven frozen routes bounded it without identifying it: it is not a simple stationary tone (phase varies, harmonics miss), not explained by the simplest auxiliary-coupled contamination (0 of 3 families passed), not recurring in the subject's six other recordings, and the linked-mastoid analysis arm is itself polluted by abnormal mastoid power. Artifact classifiers trained on labeled corpora topped out at 0.32 macro recall against a 0.55 floor. Flexible decomposition cannot name a physical source without ground truth the release does not carry.

## What still stands
A receipt-bound characterization: persistent broadband spatial-mean activity plus a persistent regional contrast with an exceptional 40-Hz share, in one identified file, surviving transient and simple-artifact rivals. Ceiling: description of one recording; no consciousness claim.

## What would revive it
Ground truth: the acquisition metadata for that recording session (amplifier, reference wiring, impedance logs), or a source-labeled independent-component library (brain/muscle/eye/heart/line/channel-noise classes) big enough to fingerprint the component.

## Card fields (G18 contract)
- plain_meaning: One night of public sleep-brainwave data contains a steady 40-hertz signal we could bound but not identify. It sits at the exact frequency people associate with awareness, and it could just as easily be a loose electrode.
- what_it_is_not: Not evidence of a consciousness signature, because muscle, electrode, and equipment sources were narrowed but never excluded; and not dismissed noise, because it survived the simple-artifact explanations that usually kill such signals.
- next_decisive_test: Fingerprint the component against a source-labeled IC library, or obtain the session's acquisition metadata and test the reference-inheritance explanation directly.
- what_we_need: DREAM-database contributors' acquisition metadata for the flagged session, or any large source-labeled EEG independent-component library.
